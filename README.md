## About Me

Love playing and creating games. I have 4+ years of industry experience as a UE developer, QA and a producer with 10+ projects. I have built multiplayer systems (with Steam), modular gameplay features, AI (with behaviour trees), UI/UX, QA automations, test cases and have lead small teams of Code and QA. Worked on both gameplay implementation and team workflow optimization. My goal is to one day create a game as beautiful as *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*.
